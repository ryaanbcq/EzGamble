How it works
  • Before the rolls, your host shows SHA-256(server seed). Another seed would give another hash, so it can't be swapped afterwards.
  • Each draw of a round is HMAC-SHA256(server seed, "client seed:round:draw"), first 52 bits divided by 252: a number n in [0, 1).
  • Roulette floor(n0 × 37). Coinflip heads if n0 < 0.5. Crash floor(96 / (1 - n0)) / 100, between x1 and x1000.
  • Mines: a Fisher-Yates shuffle of the 25 tiles, the bombs are its first tiles (numbered 1 to 25, left to right, top to bottom).
  • Crates: n0 × (sum of the chances) walks the prize table in order. Blackjack: one round shuffles the whole shoe the same way, cards are dealt from the end.
