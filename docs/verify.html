EzGamble provably fair

Paste the reveal your host gave you in chat. This page recomputes every round in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

How it works
  • Before the rolls, your host shows SHA-256(server seed). A different seed would give a different hash, so it can't be swapped afterwards.
  • Each round uses one number per draw: HMAC-SHA256(key = server seed, message = "client seed:round:draw"), first 52 bits divided by 252, a number in [0, 1).
  • Roulette: floor(n0 × 37). Coinflip: heads if n0 < 0.5. Crash: floor(96 / (1 - n0)) / 100, at least x1, at most x1000.
  • Mines: a Fisher-Yates shuffle of the 25 tiles (draw k picks among the first 25 - k tiles), the bombs are the first tiles of the shuffle. Tiles are numbered 1 to 25, left to right, top to bottom.
  • Crates: n0 × (sum of the chances) walks the prize table in order. Blackjack: one round shuffles the whole shoe the same way, cards are dealt from the end.
