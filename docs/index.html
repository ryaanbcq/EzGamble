EzGamble

Your casino table, inside your Minecraft menu.

A Fabric mod for 1.21.11. Players pay you, the mod counts their chips, you run the games, it shows who to pay back.

Your profit, live.

Every bet counts the moment it lands. Profit, volume, win rate and the session's curve, one key away.

Every bet, on record.

Games, payments and payouts in one list, filtered by wins or losses. Sessions are saved, so yesterday is still there.

Chips from /pay.

When a player pays you, the mod turns it into chips in their name. Pick a table, bet their chips, and it keeps the count.

Always on screen.

Your balance, the session's profit and every payment that comes in, on the HUD, where you put it. Off when gambling mode is off.

Crown your high rollers.

The biggest wagers stand on a podium as pixel chibis of their own skins. The winner gets the crown. Remove anyone from the database in two clicks.

Fair, and you can check.

Every outcome comes from a secret seed you commit to before the first roll. Nothing is whispered for you: you copy, you paste.

1

Before

Copy the seed's hash and paste it in chat. It can't change after this.

2

During

A viewer can give you a word as client seed. Each round draws its numbers from both.

3

After

Reveal the seed. Anyone can recompute every round and match the hash.
