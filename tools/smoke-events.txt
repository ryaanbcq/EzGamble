# A 5-minute wager race with a 100M pool over three players, its live page and HUD banner, the forced end, the podium
# finale and the results with the Pay buttons, the race in the past list, then a Double XP that doubles the VIP XP.
size 1280 800
gui 2
mode on
paid Alpha 500M
paid Bravo 2B
paid Charlie 100M
event race 5 100M
wager Alpha 2M 6
wager Bravo 10M 4
wager Charlie 1M 3
eventstate
wait 10
open
sub events
wait 20
shot events-live
close
wait 10
shot events-hud
eventend
eventstate
wait 40
shot events-finale
wait 140
shot events-results
# the first winner's Pay button (results card, first row, right side): one /pay, left waiting in the offline world
press-button Pay $50M
wait 10
shot events-results-sending
open
sub events
wait 20
shot events-past
eventstate
# Double XP: two more 2M bets of Alpha count twice in his VIP XP (12M XP before the event, 20M after)
event xp 5
wager Alpha 2M 2
vipstate Alpha
eventstate
wait 10
shot events-xp-live
close
wait 10
shot events-xp-hud
quit
