# Achievements smoke test: ./tools/smoke.sh tools/smoke-badges.txt
# Payments and wagers unlock several badges, the All players pills, the profile row, the catalogue grid, then a streak
# unlocked with the menu closed shows the HUD slide.
size 1280 800
gui 2
mode on
paid Alpha 500M
paid Alpha 100M
paid Bravo 2B
# Alpha: 120 x 1M at Blackjack (First Blood, Centurion, Marathon) and one 150M bet (Whale).
# Bravo: 20 x 60M = 1.2B (First Blood, Big Spender, Platinum so Loyal, High Roller). Bravo paid the most: first row of All players.
wager Alpha 1M 120
wager Alpha 150M 1
wager Bravo 60M 20
badges Alpha
badges Bravo
wait 20
open
chipswho all
wait 20
shot badges-all
# the first row (Bravo): his profile with the badges row under the loyalty row
click @100 @116
wait 20
shot badges-profile
# the badges row (y0 + 178, 16 px tall): the catalogue grid
click @100 @186
wait 20
shot badges-grid
close
wait 10
# 7 Coinflip wins in a row with the menu closed: Hot Streak then Lucky Seven slide down from the top of the screen
wagerwin Bravo 5M 7
wait 15
shot badges-toast
badges Bravo
wait 60
quit
