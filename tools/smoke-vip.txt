# VIP tiers: three players wager their way to different tiers, the ladder, a profile with cashback, the tier-up banner
size 1280 800
gui 2
mode on
paid Alpha 500M
paid Bravo 2B
paid Charlie 100M
# Alpha: 30 x 1M = 30M wagered (Silver). Bravo: 20 x 30M = 600M (Platinum, 1% then 1.5% cashback owed). Charlie: 5 x 1M (Bronze)
wager Alpha 1M 30
wager Bravo 30M 20
wager Charlie 1M 5
vipstate Alpha
vipstate Bravo
vipstate Charlie
wait 20
open
chipswho vip
wait 20
shot vip-ladder
# the first row of the ladder (Bravo): his profile with the cashback button
click @100 @116
wait 20
shot vip-player
vippaid Bravo 1M
wait 10
shot vip-player-paid
vipstate Bravo
close
# a tier-up while the menu is closed: Charlie crosses Silver, the banner shows under the top of the screen
wager Charlie 25M 1
wait 15
shot vip-tierup
wait 60
quit
