# Progressive jackpot: the HUD counter, the Games > Jackpot view, a forced hit with its finale, the hit in the history
size 1280 800
gui 2
mode on
paid Alpha 500M
paid Bravo 200M
# a pot worth looking at, then bets that feed it (1% of each) and draw at it (1 in 20 000 per $1M, next to never here)
jackpotset 50M
wager Alpha 5M 10
wager Bravo 2M 10
jackpot
close
wait 30
shot jackpot-hud
open
sub jackpot
wait 20
shot jackpot-view
close
# the forced hit: the pot into Alpha's chips, the finale over the whole screen
jackpothit Alpha
wait 30
shot jackpot-finale
wait 120
chips Alpha
jackpot
open
sub jackpot
wait 20
shot jackpot-history
quit
